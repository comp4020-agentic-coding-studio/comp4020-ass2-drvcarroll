Dr. Whitfield completed his doctorate in computer science at Carnegie Mellon
University before a research placement with the United States National
Security Agency, the specifics of which remain classified. He joined Slop
University's Cybersecurity Research Group after several years in
private-sector threat research.

His current work addresses execution triggers, persistence mechanisms and the
detection engineering built to counter both, and he tutors Weeks 1 through 6
alongside Professor Al-Fulani.
