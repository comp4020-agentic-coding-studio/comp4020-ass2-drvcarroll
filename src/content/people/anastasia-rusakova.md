Dr. Rusakova completed her doctorate in offensive security at Novosibirsk
State Technical University before an early posting with the GRU's technical
directorate, the remaining details of which are not publicly documented. She
joined Slop University after relocating to Australia in 2019.

Her current work addresses distribution infrastructure, command-and-control
tradecraft and the operational-security lapses that lead such infrastructure
to be attributed after the fact, and she tutors Weeks 7 through 12 alongside
Professor Sidorov.
