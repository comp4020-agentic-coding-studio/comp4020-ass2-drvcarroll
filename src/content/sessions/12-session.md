There is no laboratory exercise this week. The final session connects
eleven weeks of separate stages into a single lifecycle and prepares
students for the examination.

## Before the session

Students should bring their progress on Assignment 2 and a note of
whichever lifecycle stage still feels least secure, since the lecture is
structured to accommodate exactly that kind of question.

## In the session

The lecture recaps every stage in sequence, from idea through architecture,
execution, infiltration, persistence, distribution, and command and
control, before adding the analysis, detection and operational-security
perspectives that sit on top of them. It closes with a reminder of
Assignment 2's requirements and the final examination's two-hour,
in-person written format.

## Afterwards

Students should be able to explain how a decision at any one stage
constrains the stages around it: an architectural choice shaping what an
analyst subsequently finds, or an operational-security failure nullifying
otherwise sound technical work. Assignment 2 and the final examination are
what remains of the semester.
