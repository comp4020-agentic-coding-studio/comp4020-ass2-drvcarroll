No lab this week. The last session is for connecting eleven weeks of
separate stages into one lifecycle, and preparing for the exam.

## Before the session

Bring your Assignment 2 progress and a list of whichever lifecycle stage
still feels shakiest — the lecture is built to be interrupted with exactly
that kind of question.

## In the session

The lecture recaps every stage in order — idea, architecture, execution,
infiltration, persistence, distribution, command and control — then the
analysis, detection and operational-security perspectives on top of it,
closing with a reminder of Assignment 2's requirements and the final
examination's two-hour, in-person written format.

## Afterwards

You should be able to explain how a decision at any one stage constrains
the stages around it — architecture shaping what analysis finds, OPSEC
failures nullifying otherwise-sound technical work. Assignment 2 and the
final examination are what remains of the semester.
