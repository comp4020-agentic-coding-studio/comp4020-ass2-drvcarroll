This is the last content week before review, and the one Assignment 2 draws
on most heavily: its report requires precisely the reasoning taught in this
session, namely attribution, infrastructure hygiene, and where human error
becomes visible in the logs.

## Before the session

Students should bring whatever progress has been made on Assignment 2,
since this week's content is the last piece its report requires before the
exam-preparation window opens.

## In the session

The lecture covers attribution evidence and its associated confidence
levels, infrastructure hygiene, the metadata that persists after a
campaign's other artefacts are gone, and the recurring human-error patterns
behind well-documented operational-security failures. Lab 10 then asks
students to reconstruct a fictional actor from logs, metadata and
infrastructure evidence, and to assess the resulting attribution with a
stated confidence level rather than a guess presented as certainty.

## Afterwards

Assignment 2 asks students to infect an assigned target, retrieve its
flag, and report on open-source intelligence, reconnaissance, infection,
persistence, communication and detection avoidance, covering everything
this week's lecture and laboratory session address from the defender's
side. It is due after Week 12, within the exam-preparation window.
