This is the last content week before review, and the one Assignment 2 leans
on most: its report asks for exactly the reasoning this session teaches —
attribution, infrastructure hygiene, and where human error shows up in the
logs.

## Before the session

Bring whatever progress you have made on Assignment 2 — this week's content
is the last piece its report needs before the exam-prep window.

## In the session

The lecture covers attribution evidence and its confidence levels,
infrastructure hygiene, the metadata that outlives the artefacts a campaign
leaves behind, and the recurring human error patterns behind famous
operational-security failures. Lab 10 then has you reconstruct a fictional
actor from logs, metadata and infrastructure evidence, and assess the
attribution with a stated confidence level rather than a guess dressed up as
certainty.

## Afterwards

Assignment 2 asks you to infect an assigned target, retrieve its flag, and
report on OSINT, reconnaissance, infection, persistence, communication and
avoidance without getting caught — everything this week's lecture and lab
cover from the defender's side. It is due after Week 12, in the exam-prep
window.
